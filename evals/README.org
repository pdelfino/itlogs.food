#+TITLE: evals · what can be checked about the reader and the loop

* What runs today
=make replay= (=replay.py=) feeds the reads of real sessions (=traces/=) to today's decision logic, with no key, no glasses and no network, and exits 1 on any difference. CI runs it. It tests the decisions made from reads, not the reading itself.

=recorded-runs.org= lists every recorded session with its reads, latency and rows, and =recorded-latency.json= holds the raw timings. =trace_from_recording.py= generates them and the traces from the recordings, which stay out of git. Traces hold food names, grams, confidence and timing: no photos and no speech.

* What does not exist yet
Accuracy against ground truth. =sets/= is empty, so =make eval= prints that it has no sets. The numbers it is built to print are food top-1, grams within 1 g, and how often the loop logs without asking.

** A set
One folder per weighing under =sets/=, named =YYYY-MM-DD-<food>-<n>/=:
- =a.jpg=, =b.jpg=: two photos of the same food on the scale, as the glasses took them. Never committed.
- =truth.json=: ={"food": "cooked white rice", "grams": 87.0, "scale": "Renpho", "by": "a person reading the display", "date": "2026-11-02"}=. Committed.
- =reads.json=: written by a live run, the model's two reads, so the loop can be re-scored offline. Committed.

=make eval= replays =reads.json= with no model calls. =make eval-live= runs the photos through the configured provider (=ITLOGS_PROVIDER=meta=, =ITLOGS_VISION=meta|baseten|local=) and prints latency per read too.
