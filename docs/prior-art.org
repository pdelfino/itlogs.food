#+TITLE: What Meta has said about food logging on its glasses, and the prior art
#+DATE: 2026-NOVEMBER-04

* Meta's own feature, announced 2026-MARCH-31
Source: Meta's post on the new Ray-Ban Meta line, https://www.meta.com/blog/ray-ban-meta-styles-prescription-lenses/. Coverage: https://www.roadtovr.com/meta-health-food-logging-ray-ban-smart-glasses/ and https://9to5google.com/2026/04/06/metas-smart-glasses-will-watch-what-you-eat-to-track-nutrition-data/.

Meta wrote:
- "With a simple voice prompt or quick photo, you can log what you eat hands-free, and Meta AI will extract key nutrition details and add them to your food log in the Meta AI app."
- "This will be available to users aged 18+ in the U.S. on Ray-Ban Meta and Oakley Meta glasses soon, and Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses later this summer."
- "In the future, we'll get to a point where your AI glasses can understand what you're eating and automatically log your food."

In practice, according to the press, not Meta:
- Portions are estimated from the photo and nothing is weighed: https://www.tomsguide.com/ai/i-stopped-weighing-my-food-and-used-ray-ban-meta-glasses-to-track-calories-instead
- The log lives in the Meta AI app. Road to VR notes it does not tie into Apple Health or Samsung Health.
- US only, so it was not available in Canada as of 2026-NOVEMBER-01.

* The gap
Meta's feature answers what was eaten, and estimates how much. Four popular photo apps logged calories and fat about one-third too low across about 100 meals: https://www.westnewsmagazine.com/health/ai-calorie-tracking-apps-may-be-inaccurate-tests-find/article_fd5c470b-6cc5-5240-bdaf-c38366e01239.html. A scale removes the portion problem, and many kitchens already have one.

* Prior art
| What | Weighs? | Glasses? | Apple Health? | Notes |
|------+---------+----------+---------------+-------|
| Meta AI food logging | no, estimates | yes | no | US only, Meta AI app |

| SnapTrack: Camera Scale Reader (iOS) | reads a body scale's display | no | writes weight | no food |
| Qalzy, https://qalzy.com | yes, its own scale with a camera on a stalk | no | yes, by its site | on sale; you press a button on the scale to scan; read 2026-NOVEMBER-04 |
| Smart nutrition scales (Etekcity, Renpho) | yes, by Bluetooth | no | some | the food is picked by hand in the app |
| Nutrition5k (Google, 2021) | a dataset of weighed ingredients | no | no | research |

We did not find glasses, any scale's display and Apple Health in one hands-free loop. The pieces exist on their own.
