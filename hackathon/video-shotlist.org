#+TITLE: The three-minute video: shot list and narration
#+DATE: 2026-NOVEMBER-03

The participant guide: a demo video of up to three minutes showing only hackathon-built work, a
primary judging artifact. Recorded today, cut in Le Video Editor, uploaded to YouTube
(unlisted is enough; the link must open logged out). Issue #5.

* Setup (fifteen minutes before the first take)
- The Mac: =make stop && source ~/.secure_env_vars && make run-live= in iTerm2, font size up, the
  window narrow so FRAMES, READ, ENTRY and SAY lines are readable on a phone screen.
- The iPhone on a stand, unlocked, Settings open to check the build line, then the weighing screen.
  QuickTime on the Mac records the phone screen over USB (File, New Movie Recording, the camera
  source set to the iPhone). This is the clean capture of the big number, the chime moment and the
  table.
- A second phone (or Cadence) films Pedro at the counter wearing the glasses, from the side, so the
  scale's display, the hands and the glasses are in one frame. Landscape, 1080p.
- Foods on the counter, in the order of the script: an apple, a banana, a bowl, a bag of oats.
  Scale on, tared, display clear of glare (no lamp straight above it).
- Glasses charged, connected in the Meta AI app, the app registered. One dry run of Start, apple,
  Stop before recording, so the Ready line arrives within three seconds on camera.
- Voice: a premium English voice downloaded on the phone (Settings, Accessibility, Spoken
  Content, Voices), so the ear is not the compact robotic one (#18).

* Rules to keep
- Only hackathon-built work on screen: no itlogs.fit, no other scale, no other product.
- The glasses' own camera cannot record while the app streams: the glasses' point of view is the
  phone's preview or the frames the server kept (=photos/= in the recording folder), not a native
  capture.
- Say "logger", never "coach"; no advice, no diet, nothing about what to eat.
- Pedro's kitchen is the set; nothing with a name or an address in frame.
