Allowed. The Official Rules: the judging criteria "apply only to projects submitted during the Hackathon, not to applications"; nothing ties the submission to the application's field 7. * Positioning (the anti-project list)
"AI Nutrition Coach" and "Simple Image Analyzers" are on the guide's anti-project list. itlogs.food is neither if it stays a *hands-free logger*: a wearable sensor that writes down what was weighed. Words to use: log, record, read, weigh, hands-free, check. Words never to use, in code, README, video or Discord: coach, advice, diet plan, what to eat, healthy.

* Models named by the guide
Meta Model API: Muse Spark series, Muse Wonder, Muse Image, Muse Voice Transcribe, SAM 3.1. Open weights: Muse Glimmer, Muse Spark Open (Baseten id =meta-models/muse-spark-open-1026=, Modal, Fireworks).
